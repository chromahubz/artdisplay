## Recommended Image Specifications

- **Resolution**: Minimum 2000x2000px (for crisp quality)
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1 (square) or 4:3 works best
- **Style**: Clean, minimal backgrounds work best
- **Lighting**: Even lighting without harsh shadows

## Example Search Results to Download

### Classic Frame
1. Search: "wooden picture frame white wall"
2. Look for: Traditional gold/wooden frame on white/cream wall
3. Download in highest resolution

### Modern Frame
1. Search: "black frame modern interior"
2. Look for: Sleek black frame on dark or minimalist background
3. Download in highest resolution

### Gallery Wall
1. Search: "empty gallery wall museum"
2. Look for: Clean white wall with good lighting
3. Download in highest resolution

## How to Add Images to Project

1. Download images from above sources
2. Optimize images (compress to ~500KB using tinypng.com)
3. Place in `/public/mockups/` folder:
   - `frame1.jpg` - Classic frame
   - `frame2.jpg` - Modern frame
   - `gallery-wall.jpg` - Gallery wall

4. Images will automatically be used by the app
